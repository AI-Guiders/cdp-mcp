## Question from operator
Do User Stories give everything compactly for the SSCAD automation card?

## Take
- Yes as *shape*: who / want / why (+ short AC) is one-glance and close to Lean «concentrated input».
- No as *guarantee*: classic US often collapse to «As a проектировщик I want automation so that faster» — same water as «обеспечивать», just in agile costume.
- Overlap risk with already-chosen pieces: concept one-pager ≈ why/elevator; Система должна/не должна ≈ concrete behaviors; optional UX metaphors ≈ CustDev conclusions. A US that restates all three becomes a fourth essay.
- Best fit if used as *one slot* that binds role+goal+motive, then AC bullets are the must-list (verbs), and UX section stays optional when kettle-shared knowledge is missing — not a full backlog of stories.
- Anti: epic trees, INVEST theater, Gherkin walls in the проектировщик card.

## Lean rule
One story (or ≤3) per utility card, concrete; empty motive → rewrite or drop.
