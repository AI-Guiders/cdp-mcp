## Learning

## Operator on why UserStory fails in practice
People often don’t understand what a User Story *is* or how to use it. Thinking is lazy: saw the template → copy-paste → skipped the layer of *why this form exists* and *why it is shaped this way*.

## Implication for SSCAD card
- Empty «As a… I want… so that…» is not a UserStory problem — it’s cargo-cult of the form without the understanding layer (same pathology as ГОСТ канцелярит).
- Lean echo: «не делать без понимания» applies to the *template author and filler* too — if the card doesn’t force the why-layer, people will skip it.
- Design consequence: either teach the why in one glance (tiny legend / anti-examples), or prefer fields that can’t be filled without understanding (concrete verbs, эталон, UX conclusions) over a ritual phrase pattern.
- US still usable if the card makes the motive slot non-skippable and shows a bad vs good example inline.
